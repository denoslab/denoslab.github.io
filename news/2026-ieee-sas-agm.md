CALGARY. Members of DENOS Lab spent Thursday evening with the local engineering community at the 2026 Annual General Meeting (AGM) of the IEEE Southern Alberta Section, held Nov. 19 at the Carriage House Hotel and Conference Centre in Calgary.

Lab director [Dr. Steve Drew](/team/steve-drew/) attended with PhD students [Yunkai Bao](/team/yunkai-bao/), [Jialin Yang](/team/jialin-yang/), and [Guojun Tang](/team/guojun-tang/), MSc student [David Anez](/team/david-anez/), and Mary Xu.

The AGM is the section's yearly gathering for IEEE members and their guests. It looks back on what the section, its technical chapters, and its affinity groups did over the past year and sets out what is planned for 2027.

The evening opened with networking, followed by reports from the section chair and treasurer and a dinner. Award presentations and reports from the technical chapters, affinity groups, and student branches rounded out the program.

For the lab, whose members publish and present regularly at IEEE venues, the night was a chance to meet engineers and researchers from across southern Alberta outside the conference circuit. Thank you to the IEEE Southern Alberta Section volunteers for organizing the evening.
