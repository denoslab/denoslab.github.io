Leo Wei was a Master's student at DENOS Lab from 2023 to 2025, co-supervised by Dr. Na Li. His research on federated learning for supply chain demand forecasting brought privacy preserving machine learning to real forecasting problems. He is now AI Technical Lead at Citizen Relations, a public relations agency headquartered in Toronto with offices around the world.
