Isha Goyal spent her Women in Science and Engineering (WISE) internship at DENOS Lab building CardiCare, an artificial intelligence patient assistant for people living with coronary artery disease (CAD). CardiCare answers only from clinician approved content and refuses everything else, running entirely on an 8 GB NVIDIA Jetson so patient questions never leave the device. She is a graduate of the Renert School.
