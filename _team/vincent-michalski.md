Dr. Vincent Michalski is a postdoctoral researcher at DENOS Lab working on representation learning and reasoning. His earlier work spans video understanding, multimodal emotion recognition, visual reasoning, and conversational recommendation, and includes the Something-Something video database and the Theano framework, both widely used across the machine learning community.
