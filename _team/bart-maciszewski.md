Bart Maciszewski is a PhD student at DENOS Lab and Head of Analytics at the Imperial Oil Upstream Digital Accelerator. He holds a Bachelor of Computer Engineering and a Master of Engineering in Industrial Engineering from the University of Toronto, and brings more than twenty years across software engineering, data architecture, systems analysis, data science, operations research, and AI to his research on applying large language models to data engineering work.
