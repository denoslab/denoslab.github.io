Zirui Wang is a Master's student at DENOS Lab working on large language model applications. He contributes to EDSim, the lab's agentic simulator for emergency department operations, where autonomous agents model patients, staff, and workflows so that hospitals can find bottlenecks and test changes without disrupting real care.
