Zainab Saad is a Master's student at DENOS Lab working on large language model applications and federated learning. Her research builds a federated learning framework for carbon-aware container orchestration, predicting workload energy consumption while keeping sensitive operational data inside each enterprise, and she also contributes to the lab's privacy-preserving federated analysis of clinical trial data.
