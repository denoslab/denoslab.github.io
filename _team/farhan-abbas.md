Farhan Abbas joined DENOS Lab as a summer intern in 2026 and is the software developer of Starfish-FL, the federated learning component of the lab's STARFISH project for secure and transactable health data sharing. He also contributes to the lab's privacy-preserving federated analysis of multicentre stroke trial data.
