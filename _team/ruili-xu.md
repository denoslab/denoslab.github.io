Ruili Xu is an incoming MSc student at DENOS Lab, holding a BSc from the University of Toronto. Ruili first joined the lab as a summer intern in 2026 and continues as a graduate student in the Department of Electrical and Software Engineering.

Ruili's research sits in the lab's work on agentic simulation. During the internship Ruili connected [AgentEvac](/projects/agent-evac/), the lab's wildfire evacuation simulator, with OASIS, an LLM-agent framework for simulating social media platforms, so that simulated residents decide whether and when to evacuate under official announcements, peer behaviour, and platform recommendation algorithms. Preliminary results on a Halifax and Tantallon scenario suggested that the choice of underlying language model shifts evacuation timing at least as much as the recommendation policy does. Dr. Steve Drew [presented the abstract](/news/2026-ieee-smc/) at IEEE SMC 2026 in Bellevue, Washington.

The MSc research will build on that study, asking how the information a community shares with itself during a disaster changes how fast it can get out.
